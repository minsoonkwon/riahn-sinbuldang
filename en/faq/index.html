FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I make a reservation?
You can book your preferred date and time on Naver Booking. You can also contact us by phone (+82-41-622-3500) or Instagram DM (@sunmi9604).
Is parking available?
Yes. Parking in the building's basement lot is free.
How do I enter the basement parking lot?
You cannot turn into the parking entrance from the opposite lane. Please drive around the building via the main road and enter through the alley behind the building.
What are your opening hours and days off?
We are open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:00, and closed every Monday.
How much do your services cost?
Haircuts ₩5,000–₩25,000, perms and straightening ₩22,000–₩240,000, color ₩45,000–₩130,000, and treatment and scalp care ₩50,000–₩80,000. Depending on hair length, bob length adds +₩20,000~, shoulder to chest +₩30,000~, and below chest +₩50,000~ (short-cut length +₩0).
How do I find the salon?
We are on the 2nd floor of Jeongwoo Plaza, 70 Buldang 23-ro — the building with Olive Young. Across the street are the Buldang police box, Tous les Jours and Mega Coffee.
Can I book a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when you book. Each designer's specialties are on the designers page.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes. Pets are welcome.
Can I visit with my child?
Yes. Child seats are available, so you can visit comfortably with your child.
Is the salon wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The salon is wheelchair accessible.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (Tue–Sun 10:00–20:00).
