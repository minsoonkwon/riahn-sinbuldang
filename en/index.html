About

One-on-one consultation for your face shape and head shape —
RIAHN Hair Sinbuldang

RIAHN Hair Sinbuldang is located in Buldang-dong, Seobuk-gu, Cheonan, Chungcheongnam-do. Through one-on-one consultation, we suggest a range of styles including layered cuts, ash brown color and down perms.

We also care for your hair and scalp with head spa and scalp care. Pets are welcome and kids' chairs are available, so you can visit in comfort.

Opened in 2026 with 8 styling chairs, the salon is on the 2nd floor of Jeongwoo Plaza (the Olive Young building), 70 Buldang 23-ro. We are open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:00, and closed every Monday.

Why RIAHN Hair Sinbuldang

  • One-on-one consultation that considers your face shape and head shape.
  • Hair care programs including head spa and scalp care.
  • Pets welcome, kids' chairs, and wheelchair-accessible facilities.
  • Free parking in the building's basement lot.
