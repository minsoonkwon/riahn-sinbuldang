Layered cuts tailored to

At RIAHN Hair Sinbuldang, every cut begins with a one-on-one consultation that considers your face shape and head shape. Director Hyeonji specializes in layered and tassel cuts, Director Mina in layered cuts and tailored consultations for men and women, and Store Manager Juyeong in men's cuts.

If you would like waves or volume as well, see our perms & straightening page.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a layered or tassel cut that suits their face shape
  • Men looking for a cut that suits their head shape
  • Anyone who just wants a quick bangs trim (₩5,000)
  • Anyone who wants to find a flattering style through one-on-one consultation

Haircut FAQ

How much is a haircut?
A men's cut is ₩20,000 and a women's cut is ₩25,000. A bangs trim is ₩5,000.
Is there a designer who specializes in layered cuts?
Director Hyeonji specializes in layered cuts and perms, as well as tassel cuts, and Director Mina specializes in layered cuts and perms.
Is there a designer who specializes in men's cuts?
Store Manager Juyeong specializes in men's cuts and perms. If you would also like a down perm, we recommend Director Chaeyeon, who specializes in down perms tailored to head shape.
