Ash brown and tone-down color
with personal color consultation

RIAHN Hair Sinbuldang offers root color and full color in basic and premium options, as well as full bleach and full color with treatment (엘로이 클리닉).

Store Manager Juyeong specializes in tone-down color for women, and Director Mina specializes in personal color consultation and holds the Milbon Color Diploma. For care alongside your color, see treatment & scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants an ash brown or tone-down color
  • Anyone who wants to neatly touch up grown-out roots
  • Anyone who wants to find their best color through personal color consultation
  • Anyone who wants a bright color with bleach

FAQ

Hair color FAQ

How much are root color and full color?
Root color is ₩45,000 (basic) or ₩55,000 (premium), and full color is ₩80,000 (basic) or ₩100,000 (premium).
How much is bleach?
Full bleach is ₩100,000. Full color with treatment (엘로이 클리닉) is ₩130,000.
Can I get tone-down color or a personal color consultation?
Yes. Store Manager Juyeong specializes in tone-down color for women, and Director Mina specializes in personal color consultation.
