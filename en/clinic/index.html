Head spa and scalp care
in one place

RIAHN Hair Sinbuldang runs hair care programs including head spa and scalp care. Hair treatments include AYUNCHE Basic and AYUNCHE Premium, and for the scalp we offer scalp care.

Director Mina holds a scalp trichologist certificate. For care alongside a perm or color, see the perm and hair color pages.

Recommended for

  • Anyone looking for head spa or scalp care
  • Anyone who wants to care for their hair with an AYUNCHE treatment after a perm or color
  • Anyone who wants to smooth dry, frizzy hair
  • Anyone who wants advice from a designer with a scalp trichologist certificate
